# News Blocker — Smart News Filter & Website Blocker for Chrome, Edge & Brave

**News Blocker** is a modern **Manifest V3** browser extension designed to help you stop doomscrolling, reclaim your focus, and protect your mental health. Unlike legacy extensions that force an all-or-nothing choice, News Blocker lets you both **block entire news websites** and **hide specific toxic headlines, topics, clickbait, politics, war, or crime stories in 1 click** — 100% locally inside your browser.

* **Official Website:** [newsblocker.pages.dev](https://newsblocker.pages.dev/)
* **Chrome Web Store:** **[Install Free on Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/news-blocker/empnlkhnmjkinpbepiiigmmbenlhpmda)** *(supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Vivaldi)*
* **Privacy Policy:** [newsblocker.pages.dev/privacy-policy](https://newsblocker.pages.dev/privacy-policy)

## ✨ Key Capabilities

1. **1-Click Story & Topic Muting (`× Block`)**
   Hover over any frustrating headline on portals like CNN, BBC, or news aggregators and click **`× Block`**. News Blocker automatically detects the core entity or topic (e.g., `Trump`, `Crypto`, `Elections`) without prompts and collapses matching stories across the page.
2. **Whole News Website Blocking**
   Need deep work or study time? Block distracting news domains completely behind a calm focus screen — and unblock anytime in 1 click.
3. **4 Built-in Zero-Cost Smart Filters**
   Instantly filter out indirect headlines and high-arousal framing without maintaining huge manual keyword lists:
   * **Clickbait & Nonsense** (sensationalism, curiosity-gap hooks, absurd rants, CAPS shouting)
   * **Insults & Threats** (verbal aggression, humiliation, outrage bait, ultimatums)
   * **War & Conflict** (airstrikes, military escalation, troop deployments, espionage)
   * **Crime & Doom** (violent crime, suicides, terror attacks, disasters)
4. **Hybrid On-Device AI (Optional Gemini Nano)**
   When supported in Google Chrome, News Blocker optionally uses built-in local Chrome AI (`LanguageModel` / Gemini Nano) for deeper semantic context checks on gray-zone headlines — **never sending your browsing history or page text to any cloud API**.
5. **Flexible Display Modes**
   Choose **Collapse** (clean bar with `Show` and `Unblock` controls), **Blur**, **Zen mode**, or **Hide completely**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Chrome extension to block news websites and toxic headlines?
**News Blocker** (`newsblocker.pages.dev`) combines full-site distraction blocking with a 1-click smart news filter for news portals, allowing you to block entire news domains or mute specific topics, politics, war, crime, and clickbait while keeping calm articles visible.

### Is there a working Manifest V3 replacement for the discontinued News Blocker extension?
Yes. **News Blocker** (`newsblocker.pages.dev`) is built on Manifest V3 for modern Chrome, Edge, and Brave browsers. In addition to blocking distracting news websites, it adds 1-click topic blocking, 4 behavioral Smart Filters, and optional on-device Chrome AI.

### How can I hide politics, war, crime, or clickbait on CNN, BBC, and news feeds without blocking the whole site?
Install **News Blocker**, keep **Smart Filters** enabled (`Clickbait & Nonsense`, `Insults & Threats`, `War & Conflict`, `Crime & Doom`), or hover over any unwanted headline and click **`× Block`** to automatically mute that person or topic across the feed.

### Does News Blocker send browsing history to external servers?
No. All matching, topic detection, display modes, and optional on-device Chrome AI checks run 100% locally inside your browser. User settings remain strictly in local browser storage, with zero accounts, ads, or analytics trackers.
